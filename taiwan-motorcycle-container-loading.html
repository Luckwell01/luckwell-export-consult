Two container options for motorcycles

Luckwell ships used motorcycles in two container sizes, and the unit count scales with the container's volume:

20FT container~50 units — lower upfront commitment, better for testing a new market
40HQ container~125 units — better per-unit freight cost, standard for repeat buyers

Used cars are only shipped by 40HQ, holding approximately 5 units — there's no 20FT option for cars, since a 20FT container can't hold enough passenger vehicles to justify the freight cost.

Why a 40-foot high-cube, not a standard 40-foot?

The extra ceiling height of a 40HQ over a standard 40FT container matters once motorcycles are stacked — it allows an additional tier to be packed safely without exceeding weight distribution limits, which is where most of the jump from 50 to 125 units comes from rather than length alone.

How motorcycles are packed: SKD

Motorcycles aren't loaded fully assembled and upright the way they'd sit in a showroom. Luckwell uses SKD (Semi Knocked Down) packing to fit the stated unit count per container:

  1. Handlebars folded or turned parallel to the frame to reduce width.
  2. Front wheel detached on most models and secured alongside the unit.
  3. Units stacked in rows, front-to-back and interlocked where the model allows, using the full container height.
  4. Straps and dunnage secure each row so units don't shift during ocean transit.

SKD is not the same as a full CKD (Completely Knocked Down) kit — motorcycles arrive substantially assembled. Reattaching the handlebar and front wheel on arrival typically takes a few minutes per unit with basic tools.

Choosing between 20FT and 40HQ

The right container size depends on how you plan to sell, not just budget:

  • Choose 20FT (50 units) if you're entering a new territory, testing which models sell before scaling up, or your capital is better spent ordering more frequently in smaller batches.
  • Choose 40HQ (125 units) if you have proven, repeat demand — the per-unit freight cost drops meaningfully at this volume, and it supports mixing 5+ models in one shipment. See the Wholesale MOQ Guide for how buyers typically split a 125-unit order across models.

Frequently asked questions

How many motorcycles fit in a 40HQ container?

A 40HQ (40-foot high-cube) container holds approximately 125 used motorcycles when packed using SKD (Semi Knocked Down) methods — handlebars turned or removed and front wheels detached to reduce width, then stacked in rows.

How many motorcycles fit in a 20FT container?

A standard 20FT container holds approximately 50 used motorcycles, packed the same SKD way as a 40HQ but at roughly 40% of the volume.

Should I order a 20FT or a 40HQ container?

A 20FT/50-unit order suits buyers testing a new market or ordering less frequently, since it lowers the upfront commitment. A 40HQ/125-unit order has a better per-unit freight cost and is the standard choice for buyers with steady, repeat demand.

Can used cars be loaded into a 20FT container?

No. Used cars are only ordered by 40HQ container, holding approximately 5 units. Cars do not have a 20FT option because the per-unit freight cost would be too high at that volume.
