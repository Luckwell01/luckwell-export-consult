Used Motorcycle Supplier for Africa: Sourcing from Taiwan

Luckwell already ships to Ghana, Madagascar, Ivory Coast, and Nigeria. Here's why Taiwan-sourced scooters fit African markets, and what a first order looks like.

Why Taiwan as a source market

Taiwan requires mandatory annual vehicle inspection and has one of the highest scooter-ownership rates per capita in the world. That combination produces a large, well-documented used fleet — most units have a traceable service history and comparatively low mileage for their age, which is harder to verify sourcing from markets without similar inspection requirements.

Engine sizes in Luckwell's catalog run 50cc to 180cc — small-displacement, fuel-efficient scooters that match the everyday urban and semi-urban transport role motorcycles fill across most African markets, rather than the larger touring or off-road bikes some other source countries specialize in.

Starting with a smaller order

Buyers entering a new African market for the first time don't have to commit to a full 125-unit 40HQ container. The 50-unit 20FT option (see the Container Loading Guide) lowers the upfront commitment while still being a full-container, cost-efficient shipment — a common way to test which brands and models sell before scaling to larger, repeat orders.

Mixing 3–5 models in a single container, spread across a few price points, is the standard approach for buyers testing a new African market — it lets you read local demand before betting volume on one model.

Placing your first order

  • Confirm target models and quantities from the catalog.
  • Get a quotation and confirm the MOQ (see the Wholesale MOQ Guide).
  • Pay deposit — T/T bank transfer or Alibaba Trade Assurance for added payment security on a first order.
  • Receive inspection photos and grade notes before the balance is due.
  • Container ships from Kaohsiung once the balance is confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

Which African countries does Luckwell export used motorcycles to?

Luckwell currently ships used motorcycles and cars to buyers in Ghana, Madagascar, Ivory Coast, and Nigeria, alongside Middle East markets including Iraq, Pakistan, Yemen, and Oman.

Why do African importers source scooters from Taiwan?

Taiwan has mandatory annual vehicle inspection and one of the highest scooter-ownership rates in the world, producing a large, well-documented used fleet with detailed service history — plus small-displacement models (50cc–180cc) that match everyday urban and semi-urban transport needs common across African markets.

What's the shipping route from Taiwan to West African ports?

Containers load at Kaohsiung port and move by ocean freight to the buyer's destination port. Exact transit time depends on the shipping line and destination, and should be confirmed per order.

Can a first-time buyer in Africa place a smaller order to start?

Yes. The 50-unit 20FT container option lowers the entry point for buyers testing a new territory, compared to the standard 125-unit 40HQ order.
