Payment and shipping steps

1. Choose models and quantitySend us the models you want. The minimum order is 125 motorcycles per 40HQ container (50 units per 20FT).
2. Pay the depositUSD 10,000 deposit confirms the order.
3. Preparation: 2 to 4 weeksWe pull units from our Kaohsiung yard, inspect, clean, wrap and prepare them for loading.
4. Pay the balance in fullThe remaining balance must be paid in full before the container is loaded.
5. LoadingUnits are loaded and the container is sealed. We send photos and videos of the loading.
6. Sea shipment: about 45 daysTransit time by sea is about 45 days. We provide the Bill of Lading and packing list.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the deposit when ordering from Luckwell?

The deposit is USD 10,000, paid to confirm the order.

When do I pay the balance?

The balance is paid in full before the container is loaded.

How long does preparation take?

Preparing an order takes 2 to 4 weeks from the deposit: sourcing from the yard, inspection, cleaning and wrapping.

How long does shipping take?

Sea transit takes about 45 days after loading, depending on the destination port.

What is the minimum order for used motorcycles?

125 motorcycles per 40HQ container (50 units per 20FT). Used cars: 5 cars per 40HQ container.

What do used motorcycles cost?

Indicative FOB Kaohsiung prices are about USD 185 to 900 per unit; for example Yamaha Cygnus 2 USD 290, KYMCO Racing USD 245, KYMCO G5 USD 225, SYM GT USD 220, KYMCO GP USD 215. Used cars start from about USD 1,880.
