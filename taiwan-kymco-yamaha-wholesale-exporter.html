Why buyers mix KYMCO and Yamaha in one container

KYMCO and Yamaha are the two most recognized scooter brands among Luckwell's wholesale buyers, so most containers include both rather than committing to a single brand. This spreads demand risk and covers a wider price range — Yamaha's Cygnus and KYMCO's G-series are typically the fastest-moving models across buyer markets, with the larger-displacement Racing King, Force, and S MAX filling a smaller, higher-price segment.

The Cygnus (Yamaha) and G4/G5/G6 (KYMCO) lines are the closest direct competitors in the lineup — similar 125cc step-through format. Buyers often order both to test which brand performs better in their specific market before committing volume to one.

Frequently asked questions

How many Yamaha and KYMCO scooter models are available wholesale from Taiwan?

Luckwell's current catalog has 17 used Yamaha models (including 5 Cygnus variants, S MAX, GTR, and GTR Aero) and 15 used KYMCO models (including the G4/G5/G6 series, GP/GP2, and Racing King), ranging from 50cc to 180cc.

Can I order Yamaha and KYMCO models in the same container?

Yes. Mixing brands and models within one motorcycle container is standard practice — most wholesale orders combine Yamaha, KYMCO, and other brands to spread risk and match different price points in the buyer's market.

What's the biggest-displacement KYMCO or Yamaha model available?

The KYMCO Racing King at 180cc is the largest-displacement model currently available, followed by the Yamaha Force and S MAX at 155cc.

Are Yamaha and KYMCO scooters graded and priced differently?

Both brands use the same A/B/C condition grading standard. Pricing is set per unit based on model, engine size, and grade rather than by brand alone.
