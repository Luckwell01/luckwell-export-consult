The car MOQ: 5 units per 40HQ container

A standard 40HQ container holds approximately 5 passenger cars, which sets the MOQ for used car wholesale orders. Unlike motorcycles, there's no smaller 20FT option — at that volume the per-unit freight cost for cars would be too high to make sense for either side. See the Container Loading Guide for how the equivalent motorcycle math works.

Motorcycles and cars are separate orders

If you're sourcing both, note that motorcycles and cars are quoted, ordered, and loaded independently — each against its own MOQ. A container can't mix the two categories, since packing methods and volume calculations are different for each. Buyers who want both simply place two orders, which can still ship close together if timed with the supplier.

Cars are graded on the same A/B/C standard as motorcycles — see the Grading Standard Guide — so if you're comparing condition across both categories, it's one consistent scale.

Frequently asked questions

What is the MOQ for used cars exported from Taiwan?

The MOQ for used cars is 5 units per 40HQ container — a full-container quantity based on how many passenger cars fit in a 40-foot high-cube container. There is no 20FT option for cars.

What used car brands and models are available from Taiwan?

Luckwell's current used car lineup includes Hyundai (Santa Fe, i30, Matrix), Nissan (Tiida), Suzuki (Carry), and Toyota (Corolla Altis, Camry, Vios, Wish, Yaris).

Can I order motorcycles and cars in the same container?

No. Motorcycles and cars are quoted, ordered, and loaded independently, each against its own MOQ, because they use different packing methods and container space calculations.

Are used cars graded the same way as motorcycles?

Yes. The same A/B/C grading standard applies — Grade A (excellent engine, minor cosmetic wear), Grade B (good running condition, moderate cosmetic wear), Grade C (functional, visible wear, priced accordingly).
